# Infinity v3 licensing

This guide applies to `libs/infinity-v3`. Other Infinity projects retain their
own licenses.

## Choose a license

Covered original fork contributions and modifications are available under either:

- [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.txt):
  use its standard permissions for noncommercial purposes, personal uses, and
  qualifying noncommercial organizations. No additional Infinity conditions or
  platform blacklist apply under this alternative.
- [Infinity License 1.0 for Commercial Use](LICENSES/Infinity-License-1.0-for-Commercial-Use.txt):
  commercial use on owned hardware or permitted providers, subject to the fixed
  platform restrictions in Appendix A and the license's other terms.

The complete terms and rights-holder scope are in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Both alternatives are source-available licenses with use restrictions. Each
contributor grants only rights they own or are authorized to license.

## Redistribution and contributions

Follow the requirements of the license you choose. Commercial-license
redistributions must retain both alternatives, Appendix A and required notices,
identify changes, offer covered modifications under both alternatives, and
supply editable source and build/install instructions as that license requires.
Those additional requirements do not apply under the PolyForm alternative.

New covered contributions are offered under both alternatives unless different
terms are agreed before inclusion. Contributors retain ownership.

## Other material and existing grants

Inherited Text Embeddings Inference code and previously Apache-licensed
contributions retain their Apache-2.0 grants. Third-party components and
independently licensed contributions retain their own terms. Model weights,
datasets, and dependencies are not relicensed by their inclusion or use.
See [NOTICE](NOTICE), [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), and component
notices. Existing grants remain available for the material and rights they cover.

Docker distributions include `LICENSE`, `LICENSES/`, and component notices at
`/usr/share/licenses/infinity_v3/`. Native LibTorch installations include them
under `share/licenses/tei_torch/`.
